Tour Introduction

Welcome to The Laskett Garden, the creation of Sir Roy Strong and his late wife, Julia Trevelyan Oman. This audio provides an overview of what makes this garden a living work of art.

The Victoria and Albert Temple

This iconic temple was installed to commemorate Sir Roy Strong's retirement. Learn the fascinating story behind this focal point of the garden.

The Rose Garden

A vibrant and theatrical space, the rose garden is a key feature of The Laskett. This section explains the care and history of this beautiful space, which is designed to be in bloom throughout the year.
