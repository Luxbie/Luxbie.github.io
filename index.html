Our Proud History

Founded in the spring of 1952 by a small group of local neighbors, our club began as a humble seed-sharing circle on a front porch. Over the decades, we have grown into a cornerstone of the community, dedicated to the beautification of our town and the preservation of heirloom plant varieties.

Throughout the years, our members have maintained the historic rose gardens at the town library, planted hundreds of trees in local parks, and passed down invaluable, time-tested gardening wisdom from generation to generation.

While the world has changed rapidly around us, our core mission remains exactly the same as it was over seventy years ago: to foster a love of horticulture, to respect the earth, and to enjoy the simple, quiet camaraderie of fellow gardeners.

Become a Member

We welcome gardeners of all experience levels. Review our membership details below and reach out to join our historic community.

Membership Details

Annual Dues

$25.00 per year

Dues are collected every January and go directly towards our community beautification projects, seed banks, and meeting refreshments.

Benefits & Terms

  • Monthly educational meetings featuring guest speakers and master gardeners.
  • Exclusive access to the Heritage Seed Bank and our annual spring cuttings swap.
  • Members are encouraged to volunteer at least twice a year for local town planting projects.

Ready to Join?

To sign up, simply send us an email with your name and phone number. Our welcoming committee will reply with instructions for your first meeting and how to submit your annual dues.
